ReproduceCode

A Linux kernel runs below, with kSTEP holding its scheduler clock. Nothing happens until you say so: create adds a runnable task (on any CPU, or create 2 for CPU 2 only), pin moves one, kill ends one, tick advances the scheduler by exactly one tick, balance runs load balancing now instead of waiting for its interval. The timeline shows, per CPU, which task ran after each tick; the table lists the live tasks with their scheduler counters.

not booted

Space ticks once; auto tick keeps ticking at the chosen rate until paused, and other commands still work in between. Tasks are labelled t0, t1, … in creation order; the kernel knows them by pid.
